Technical merit · Gen AI is load-bearing

How Gemini is used (and kept honest)

One pass, many signals

  • Video + time-ordered stills + high-detail close-ups (item pile, hands, scanner) in a single call
  • Tool calls first: receipt, zone policy, SKU prices
  • Structured output: verdict, items with timestamps, boxes, custody (came from → ended in)
  • Separate image-only call lists the item pile; staff confirm the chips

Guard-rails around the model

  • Deterministic receipt rule and shop-floor rule can override or downgrade
  • One re-check when an answer contradicts itself; otherwise "needs review", never a guess
  • 50-second budget, model fallback on quota, results cached per clip
  • Camera memory: staff reviews become few-shot context; the Tier-1 gate auto-tunes from false-alarm rate
ShrinkSight5
Privacy & fairness by design

Built so the wrong person isn't accused

No faces leave the device

Live clips are face-blurred in the browser before upload. No facial recognition, no biometric templates.

Clips aren't stored

Only the report is kept. Your own replay copy stays in your browser for 24 h and is deleted on sign-out.

Evidence, not vibes

Every claim is timestamped and boxed; rules check the model; a human makes the final call with a reason.

Retention

False alarms deleted after 1 day, real events after 7 days. Synthetic or consented footage only.

Actions only

Prompts describe actions — never appearance, age, gender or ethnicity.
