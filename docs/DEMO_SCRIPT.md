# ShrinkSight — demo video script

**Theme:** Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences  
**Live site:** https://shrinksight-app.web.app  
**Length:** about 2:20. Export under 2:50. Public link. English. 1080p.

Read at a calm pace, about two words a second. Start the next line as the click lands. Do not hold a shot in silence.

Start on the login page, signed out. Cut any loading spinner between **Enter the demo store** and the camera tiles.

## Setup

- Fresh incognito window, 1920×1080. Hide the bookmarks bar. Page zoom about 110%.
- Open https://shrinksight-app.web.app/login before record. Do not sign in yet.
- On **Sign Up**, do not press **Create account**. That button only shows an error. The line under the title is the point.
- Do not change a role dropdown. Do not type or save in Settings.
- Click only a row tagged **Demo clip · just now**. Older rows have no video.
- Do not open **Investigate a clip** or **Demo clips**.

## 0:00–0:16 · Sign in and sign up

**0:00–0:05.** Login panel. **Open the demo**, the email and password fields, **Login**, and **Sign in with Google** are all in frame.

Say: “Sign in to the store. Email and password, or Google.”

**0:05–0:11.** Click **Sign Up**. The title becomes **Create account**. The line under it reads **Store accounts are issued by a manager.**

Say: “Sign up does not open a free account. A manager issues it.”

**0:11–0:16.** Click **Back to login**. Cursor on **Enter the demo store**. Click it on the last word.

Say: “This demo signs in as the manager. No password.”

**Caption:** Manager issues the account.

## 0:16–0:27 · Monitor

**0:16–0:20.** Tiles playing, **Demo feed** on each, skeleton on the picture.

Say: “In as the manager. The cameras are live, scan drawn on each one.”

**0:20–0:24.** New row under **Alerts & activity**, tagged **Demo clip · just now**. Chime.

Say: “That lane just flagged. Gemini has already checked this clip.”

**0:24–0:27.** Cursor to **Playback**. Click on the last word.

Say: “Open playback for that moment.”

**Caption:** Lane flagged.

## 0:27–0:44 · Playback

**0:27–0:32.** Click the result marked **demo, just now**. The player shows that clip.

Say: “This is the same clip, from the camera that just flagged.”

**0:32–0:38.** Gemini’s box on the item. Verdict chip and reason under the player.

Say: “Gemini’s box is on the item. The verdict and the reason are already written.”

**0:38–0:44.** Click one timeline mark. Picture jumps. Move to **Open the incident**.

Say: “The timeline jumps to that second. Open the full incident.”

**Caption:** Gemini’s result on the clip.

## 0:44–1:14 · Incident

**Human review** must show **Confirm** and **Dismiss**, not “Confirmed by staff · 9 Oct”. That old line is a saved review. A demo alert now opens as **Needs review** again. The model, the watching time, and the token count stay.

**0:44–0:52.** Verdict chip, loss amount, and the ticks: watched the clip, checked the till or the zone, wrote the report.

Say: “This is the alert that just landed. Gemini watched the clip, checked the store data, and wrote this report.”

**0:52–1:02.** Point at the reason, then the receipt row or the concealed item, whichever is on screen.

Say, if it is a checkout: “This item is not on the receipt. That is the loss.”

Say, if it is the floor: “It left the shelf and ended concealed. That is the alert.”

**1:02–1:14.** **Human review** shows **Confirm** and **Dismiss**. Click **Confirm**. Pick **Item not scanned** or **Item concealed**. The card then says **Confirmed by staff**, with today’s time. Under it, read the model line and the token line.

Say: “A person still decides. Confirm. The model was Gemini Flash Lite, and the token count is on the card.”

**Caption:** Staff confirm.

## 1:14–2:08 · Settings

Open **Settings** from the account menu. Click **Cameras** as the first line starts.

**1:14–1:26. Cameras.** One lane, zones drawn on its picture.

Say: “Settings, cameras. This lane, with the scanner and bagging zones drawn on its own picture. The wall scan uses these.”

**1:26–1:36. Zones.** Click **Zones**. List shows item pile, scanner, bagging, shelf, exit.

Say: “Zones are those spots. Pile, scanner, bagging, shelf, exit. A high-value shelf is marked once.”

**1:36–1:50. Rules.** Click **Rules**. Point at one saved rule and its chips: **Loitering**, the zone, **> 45 s**. Do not type.

Say: “Rules are sentences. This one is a long stay at the spirits shelf after nine. Gemini turned it into loitering, that zone, over forty-five seconds.”

**1:50–2:08. Roles.** Click **Roles**. Point at the roster, then at one **Manager** dropdown and one **User** dropdown. Do not change them.

Say: “Roles. Each person is Manager or User. They sign in with that email, or with Google. A user works the lanes. A manager keeps the cameras and the rules. One manager always stays.”

**Caption:** Manager or user.

## 2:08–2:22 · Close card

Full-frame card, no browser. Read it as it appears.

shrinksight-app.web.app

Say: “ShrinkSight. The desk flags the lane. Gemini checks the clip against the till. Live now.”
